Ham Test

Do you want to prepare for your US Amateur Radio license examination? Ham Test makes it easy.

Head on over to Study Mode, and work your way through the question pool.

Once you've seen all the questions, you are ready to take a Practice Test. If you feel like it, take a few of them. If you consistently pass, you should consider scheduling your actual exam.

Study Mode

Prepare for your exam using the power of repetition.

Practice Tests

Take a practice test and see how well you would do on the exam.

Want to study offline?

You can use Ham Test's Android app, completely offline with no internet access at all!

Best of all, you can import and export your progress through the settings, making it easy to switch between devices.

Ham Test is open source software, available on GitHub.
