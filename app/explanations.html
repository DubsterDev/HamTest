Contributing explanations

I'm trying to get explanations for all of the questions in the pools. These should be original and not be copied from another site like HamStudy, as those are not licensed for open source use. Instead, they should be your original work.

Please do not use AI to write explanations. If you're contributing explanations, you should write them yourself or have permission from the author to add them here.

Explanations submitted will be licensed under the GPL-3.0 license. Pending changes are not visible here.
